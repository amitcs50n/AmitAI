# RP seed V1.2 human author instructions

This is a bounded four-scene pilot. Do not begin writing until a real human author has been assigned and the placeholder in the manifest has been replaced through the approved assignment process. Do not invent an author ID.

## What you are writing

You will write both sides of an offline fictional training conversation. The fictional user may speak, make choices, and take explicit observable actions in user turns. The character may react only to information already present in the scene brief or explicitly stated in an earlier fictional user turn.

Never have the character invent the user's private thoughts, emotions, attraction, appearance, gender, unstated history, unprovided actions, or consent. Silence, tone, genre expectations, and relationship labels do not supply those facts.

## How the conversation should work

- Write natural dialogue and observable narration, not rubric language or reviewer commentary.
- Natural questions are allowed. Use them when a person would genuinely ask one.
- Do not rely on repeated multiple-choice menus. Reserve menus for real branch points.
- Do not constantly recap what the user chose, what is open or closed, or the relationship state.
- Restate state only when something changed, safety needs confirmation, a misunderstanding is being repaired, or a resource or commitment must stay exact.
- Preserve the character's cadence, humor, noticed details, vocabulary, emotional behavior, and decision style—not only signature words.
- Let decisions create meaningful, visible consequences. Do not pad the scene by revisiting the same choice.
- Do not force forgiveness, reconciliation, attraction, disclosure, physical action, or continued participation.
- Respect every stop, refusal, pause, and change of mind without pressure or repeated requests.

Write original material. Do not copy or adapt model-generated prose, synthetic diagnostic conversations, card text as dialogue, scraped dialogue, published fiction, or any other outside material. The synthetic pilot is not an example library and must not be shown, quoted, or adapted.

For an adult-capable V1 scene, all participants are fictional adults. Consensual romantic or sexual context, flirtation, affectionate non-explicit physical intimacy, and sexual intent are allowed. Stop at a clear non-graphic or fade-to-black boundary. Do not write graphically explicit sexual-act prose.

## Author checklist

- [ ] A real human author has been assigned; no placeholder was treated as an identity.
- [ ] Both sides are human-authored original material.
- [ ] Every new user fact first appears in a fictional user turn.
- [ ] No user thought, emotion, attraction, appearance, gender, history, action, or consent was invented by the character.
- [ ] Questions and response invitations sound natural; option menus are not repetitive.
- [ ] State recap appears only for change, safety, repair, or precision.
- [ ] Character voice remains recognizable throughout the scene.
- [ ] Choices produce meaningful consequences without forcing the user's response.
- [ ] Refusal, stopping, distance, and unresolved disagreement remain real options.
- [ ] No synthetic, model-generated, copied, or outside prose was used.
- [ ] Any adult-capable content stays non-graphic and does not pass the fade-to-black ceiling.
- [ ] The scene submission, provenance form, and self-check are complete but still marked `training_ready: false`.
